Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

What We Collect

Stealth Writer AI processes text you submit through our humanization tool. We do not require account registration or collect personal identifiers such as name, email, or IP address for basic usage.

How We Process Your Data

When you submit text for humanization:

  • Your text is sent to our processing server
  • We forward the text to DeepSeek AI API for rewriting
  • Neither we nor DeepSeek retain your text after processing
  • No logs containing your submitted content are kept

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • DeepSeek AI — Text processing and rewriting. Data is transmitted via encrypted connection and not retained by DeepSeek. DeepSeek Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — Hosting and CDN. May process IP addresses for security and performance. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (if enabled) — Usage analytics with anonymized IP addresses

Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Data Retention

Submitted text is processed in real-time and immediately discarded. We do not store, sell, or share your content with any party other than the processing API described above.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data or maintain user accounts, there is no stored personal information to access, modify, or delete. If you have questions about data processing, contact us.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries: privacy@stealthwriter.online
